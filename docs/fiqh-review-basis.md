# Hanbali review basis and unresolved contract questions

Checked 2026-10-24. These sources inform a specification; they are not approval
of this particular service. Contemporary academy resolutions are not themselves
proof of a single undisputed Hanbali position. Read actual Arabic contract terms
and the relevant doctrinal passages before claiming compliance.

1. Ibn Qudamah, *al-Mughni*, discussion of loans and suftajah:
   [Arabic text](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/15/2662/فصل-في-قرض-شرط-فيه-أن-يزيده).
   Records differing reports from Ahmad about repayment elsewhere and favors
   permission for mutual benefit without harm. This supports examining remote
   repayment; it does not establish unrestricted trading of wallet debts.
2. IIFA resolution 53 (4/6), 20 March 1990:
   [Possession and its modern forms](https://iifa-aifi.org/ar/1792.html).
   Recognizes constructive possession and specified account-credit situations.
   Effective access matters. Applying this to a new P2P balance is an inference
   requiring review, not permission created by a signed database entry.
3. IIFA resolution 84 (1/9), 6 April 1995:
   [Cash transfer and currency exchange](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/32503.html).
   Distinguishes same-currency remittance and exchange followed by remittance.
   Currency, rate, effective possession and the actual service contract matter.
4. IIFA resolution 86 (3/9), 6 April 1995:
   [Bank deposits](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/33403.html).
   Treats guaranteed repayable-on-demand current-account deposits as loans.
   The proposed service must determine whether its funding is debt, safeguarded
   custody or agency according to actual rights, not its app labels.
5. Islam Question & Answer, ruling 257731, 7 September 2018:
   [Inter-operator remittances](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/257731).
   Cites al-Mirdawi's *al-Insaf* on paying another's instruction as a loan where
   funds have not been provided, and distinguishes prefunded paid agency.
   An advance cannot earn a stipulated increment merely renamed a service fee.
   This is a published fatwa, not a substitute for checking the cited Hanbali text.

## Questions the actual contracts must resolve

- Which party owes the customer after credit issuance? Does the original dar
  remain liable alongside the backing dar, and on what contractual basis?
- Are onward payments agency instructions, valid assignments or some other
  arrangement? Avoid silently permitting discounted sale of monetary debts.
- Does the credited recipient actually have effective disposal/redemption, or
  merely an uncertain promise conditional on a later agent's discretion?
- Is each FX exchange spot with required possession, distinct from a later
  same-currency payout? Agreed rates alone do not answer this.
- Are customer and inter-dar fees genuinely for permitted services, or payment
  for lending time, advancing funds or providing a guarantee?
- What is the precise deposit/security contract (proposed rahn where suitable),
  its custodian's mandate, permitted use, realization authority and surplus rule?
- What enforcement costs are recoverable? Do not assume a punitive forfeiture,
  late-profit surcharge or percentage slashing is permissible.

The 100% same-currency safety profile is an engineering proposal. It neither
creates a fatwa nor resolves the legal custody and security arrangements.
