# Temporary transaction-bound QR approval — M6 safety prototype

This experimental crate does **not** execute transfers or produce spendable
credits. It creates/validates cryptographically signed approval evidence for
cash-in and cash-out against the exact bilateral Hawala transfer and a
mutually signed human/machine interaction.

## Two distinct QR surfaces

- Permanent `hawala:id:v1:` identifies a participant through a full
  QR-presented public key + visible name or short public code.
- Ephemeral `hawala:auth:v1:` carries only a randomly generated 32-byte
  opaque authorization reference. It does **not** contain user PII, a bank
  account identifier, private key or self-sufficient bearer withdrawal
  capability. An authorized verifier resolves the reference over a live,
  mutually authenticated Iroh peer session.

`AuthorizationRequest` is separately signed by the actual initiating
cash holder key after all displayed transaction details have been reviewed
in a trusted authenticator. The signature binds:
- exact beneficiary/sender-device holder and scanning counterparty/verifier;
- accepted bilateral terms digest and unique transfer;
- cash-in or cash-out, amount in currency minor units, disclosed fee
  in the selected currency;
- already mutually signed interaction nonce and exact operation;
- durable intended cash attempt ID, fresh verifier challenge and unique
  displayed QR nonce;
- cryptographically established channel/session binding from the peer adapter;
- issue time and a lifetime of at most 120 seconds.

Both human and machine actors can be holders or verifiers, but a machine's
claimed operator remains **untrusted** without independent current
delegation. Even a valid signature does not grant legal/financial authority.

## Verification pipeline

1. Independently scan/resolve the full intended identity of each participant.
2. Verify Iroh transport identity + QR key possession on BOTH sides.
3. Check the signed, unexpired `AgreedInteraction` and the dual-agent
   `SignedTerms`; match the **exact** cash leg, amount, currency, parties
   and route.
4. Retrieve `SignedAuthorization` from the mutually authenticated session,
   compare the signed QR nonce, fresh challenge, session digest and intended
   audience to independently obtained expected values.
5. Validate the Ed25519 signature, time window, then consume the QR nonce
   exactly once under the intended verifier/holder identity.
6. **Do not make funds available or command an ATM.** Before any real
   money operation, the nonce consumption must be part of the SAME durable
   financial transaction as collateral/funding/credit reservation, operating
   policy, eligibility, quote/fee verification and attempt fencing.

A standalone `ReplayGuard` is insufficient for step 6. Atomic consumption
with the actual execution authority and crash-safe fencing is not yet
implemented and is a hard production gate. No 0-RTT money messages.

## Safety limitations

- The provided signed fee field is **disclosed** but its economic correctness
  is not checked against a full accepted quote here. Full FX and fee quote
  validation is an independent prerequisite.
- Proof-of-possession does not prove a user read an honest screen, stood
  physically near an ATM, or held cash. Mitigate relay by session binding,
  authenticated devices, trusted display and step-up verification.
- Cryptographic nonces/challenges and the live Iroh channel digest must be
  generated by secure independent sources. The crate rejects all-zero
  values but is NOT a random-number generator or QUIC adapter.
- A QR reference can be photographed/replayed; it is not a secret or cash
  withdrawal token. Only strict per-session signed approval and a durable
  once-only financial reservation can be used for money movement.
- No identity revocation service; ephemeral *approval* expiry is not the
  revocation of a persistent identity or historical signatures.

Tests cover both cash operations, all four orientations of human/machine
counterparties, reversed initiators, wrong audience/challenge/session/QR,
incorrect amount/currency, malformed QR and replay.

**Next milestone:** move this proof into a single atomic custody/ledger
transaction, bind it to the durable human/ATM attempt journal, and test
crash and network partition around the commit boundary before connecting
actual machines.
